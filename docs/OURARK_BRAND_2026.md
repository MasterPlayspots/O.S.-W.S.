# OurArk — a clearer beginning

## Positioning

OurArk is a software and AI studio that builds useful tools and helps clients turn complex workflows into practical software. The website has two equally clear destinations: explore a shipped product, or discuss a custom project.

Brand promise: **Bring clarity to complex work.**

Product architecture:
- **OurArk** — the studio and parent brand, at ourark.io.
- **O.A. - O.S** — the free MIT-licensed local workspace. Code, imported session metadata, usage and read-only JSON memory. Available through the public repository.
- **O.A.-Connector** — a planned connection service. No paid model access, provider partnership, ready OAuth flow or reseller rights are implied.

## Visual direction

Warm white, graphite and quiet green. Large, direct typography; short passages; one useful action at a time; actual product screenshots. Borrow Apple's restraint, hierarchy and attention to detail, not its logo, proprietary imagery, type assets or page layouts.

| Token | Value | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| Canvas | #F7F8F5 | Open, low-noise marketing background |
| Surface | #FFFFFF | Product frames and readable content |
| Ink | #182421 | Main text and dark sections |
| Muted ink | #56635E | Secondary copy, still readable |
| Deep teal | #176B5B | Main action on light surfaces |
| Pale mint | #D8ECE5 | Quiet supporting panels |
| Mint | #8DD7BA | Action and focus accent on dark UI |
| Dark canvas | #101816 | Workspace background |
| Dark surface | #19231F | Workspace cards |
| Warm sand | #E8DDCB | Occasional editorial supporting surface |

These are design intentions, not universal psychological effects or proven conversion improvements. Color associations depend on context, culture, lightness and saturation. Validate the direction through comprehension and usability checks; never promise that a color creates trust or increases sales by itself.

Status colors retain text labels. Brand teal is not proof that a source is connected. Red is reserved for errors; amber for attention or unknown/stale status. Focus and readable contrast take precedence over visual subtlety.

## Type and motion

Use the visitor's system sans-serif stack. No remote font requests. Display: 56–92 px on desktop, 40–52 px on small screens, close tracking and comfortable line length. Body: 17–20 px on the website; dense workspace controls remain smaller but legible. Avoid imitation Apple SF font distribution.

Motion is optional, short and tied to interaction. No automatic rotating hero, moving code windows, forced scroll, background video or looping decorative animation. Honor reduced-motion preferences.

## English voice

Plain, specific, human. Use “Available”, “Planned”, “Demo data” and “Not connected” consistently. Describe software behavior before architecture. No invented customers, testimonials, savings, partnerships, launch dates or security certifications. Agency capabilities are offered services, not claims of past client results.

The public dashboard and screenshots use English. The historical UNIFORCE commit remains intact. Existing personal notes and imported source content must never be automatically rewritten or translated. Model names, IDs, hashes and source metadata remain exact.

## Homepage narrative

1. Studio introduction: what we build and for whom.
2. O.A. - O.S: real English product view and free source download.
3. Three useful workflows: arrange work, inspect connections, carry context.
4. Agency: discover, prototype, connect, hand over.
5. Shipping ledger: available workspace; planned connector; deliberate next steps.
6. FAQ: cost, hosting, real connections, client work.
7. Contact: an explicit email action, not a fake booking or unconnected form.
